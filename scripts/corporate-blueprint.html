💡 The Killer Differentiator: Automated Academic On-Duty (OD)
Traditional platforms (Unstop, Devfolio) lose 60%+ of physical attendance because students cannot get leave approval from their colleges. EventPulse directly connects the Hackathon check-in scanner to the university HOD's dashboard. When a student scans their pass, their HOD receives a verified OD request with a single "Grant OD" button. Students and colleges will choose EventPulse over any third-party portal for this feature alone.
